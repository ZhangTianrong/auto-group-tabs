# Privacy Policy for TabGroup Automata

Last updated: 2026-04-15

TabGroup Automata is a Microsoft Edge extension that helps organize tabs into tab groups based on user-defined rules, including regex rules, domain-based grouping, and expand/collapse behavior.

## What data the extension accesses

To provide its functionality, the extension accesses tab and tab group information in Microsoft Edge, which can include:

- tab URLs
- tab titles
- tab group names and colors
- window and tab state needed for grouping behavior
- extension settings that you configure

## How data is used

The extension uses this information only to provide its tab grouping features inside Microsoft Edge.

- Tab and tab group information is processed locally in the browser.
- Settings are stored using Microsoft Edge extension storage APIs.
- The extension does not require an account.

## Data sharing

TabGroup Automata does not sell your data.

TabGroup Automata does not intentionally transmit your tab data, browsing data, or settings to external servers for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

## Data retention

Your saved configuration remains in your browser's extension storage until you change it, remove it, or uninstall the extension.

## Third-party services

The extension is distributed through browser extension stores and may link to its source code repository or support pages, but its core tab grouping functionality does not rely on third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Your choices

You can:

- edit or delete your grouping rules at any time
- remove the extension from your browser at any time
- clear extension storage by removing the extension or resetting its settings
